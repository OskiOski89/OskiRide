# Routes and graphics

## Choose a route

Open **Ride** and select **Alpine Pass**, **Alpe d’Huez**, **Winding Switchbacks**, **Interval Valley**, or **Flat Time Trial**. Alpe d’Huez is a GPX-based HC climb with 21 numbered bends and a listed distance of about 13.9 km. The other courses cover mountain, technical, rolling, and flat terrain.

Select **Import GPX** or drag a file into the window. OskiRide builds the route from the track, smooths elevation, derives grades, and detects climbs. **My GPX routes** lets you reopen, rename, favorite, search, and delete saved routes. Identical file contents map to the existing route. **Ride history** opens the journal for that course.

Try importing a synthetic 4.3 km route with a rolling elevation profile. [Sample GPX and import steps](Installation.md#try-a-sample-gpx)

Use **Start location** or the elevation profile to choose a starting point before riding. The ride display includes a minimap, a full elevation profile, the grade ahead, climb categories, and turn and climb alerts. GPX timestamps can drive a pace ghost. [Riding and ghosts](Features.md#riding-controls-and-ghosts)

## Set up your view

- Choose a chase, handlebar, or overhead camera with **C**. **Field of view** adjusts the base camera angle, and speed effects appear on fast descents or during sprints.
- **Settings → Compare rider models** opens Rider Studio. Preview **Velocity**, **Alpine**, **Nocturne**, and **Heritage** side by side. The preview supports pose changes, cadence, rotation, and zoom.
- **Time of day** offers day, day to sunset, sunset, and night. **Weather and road surface** offers dry and reflective wet roads, rain, and alpine snow.
- **Graphics quality** offers **Low**, **Standard**, and **High**. Adaptive graphics can temporarily reduce shadows and scenery range. The FPS counter shows how the scene runs on your system.

The 3D world includes PBR lighting and reflections, tunnels, mountains, forests, buildings, road signs, LED start and finish gates, and 12 animated spectator models. Riders animate pedaling, coasting, standing sprints, and cornering lean. **Alpe d’Huez crowd audio** controls the crowd heard near bend 7. [Complete feature guide](Features.md)
