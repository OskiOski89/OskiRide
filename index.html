            Before you start

            • Warm-up: Ride for about 8–10 minutes so the trainer reaches operating temperature.
            • Tire (wheel-on trainers): Check tire pressure (7–8 bar) and roller tension.
            • Calibration: Accelerate the trainer to 37.0 km/h, then stop pedaling completely.
