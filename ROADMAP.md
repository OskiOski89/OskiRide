# OskiRide roadmap

## Near-term priorities

- **Test a broad range of physical cycling equipment on Windows.** Cover as many commonly used Bluetooth trainer, sensor, and controller models as possible. Start with FTMS, Tacx FE-C, and Wahoo Direct trainers, power meters, speed and cadence sensors, heart-rate monitors, and Zwift Click V1 and V2. Record the model, firmware, Windows version, pairing and telemetry results, and SIM or ERG response where supported. Publish the results in the compatibility guide. [Hardware compatibility](wiki/en/Equipment.md#hardware-compatibility) · [Test my trainer](https://github.com/OskiOski89/OskiRide/issues/new?template=trainer-test.yml)
- **Reproduce GPS routes as faithfully as possible.** Compare each generated 3D ride with the source GPX track for distance, road alignment, bends, elevation, and gradients. Measure differences on routes with tight switchbacks, long climbs, and rolling terrain. Tune smoothing so GPS noise is reduced while real bends and changes of grade remain recognizable. [GPX routes and graphics](wiki/en/Routes-and-Graphics.md)
- **Make the first ride easier.** Use feedback from new riders to improve the steps for pairing a trainer, checking its readings, and starting the sample GPX route. [Installation and first ride](wiki/en/Installation.md#first-ride)
- **Improve ride recovery.** Prioritize reproducible reports about interrupted rides, Bluetooth reconnects, and resuming a workout. Use those reports to expand the regression checks around session recovery. [Troubleshooting and tests](wiki/en/Troubleshooting.md#run-the-regression-tests)
